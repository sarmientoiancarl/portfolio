How I Do Things

A process built to avoid mistakes, not just move fast.

01

I document repeatable tasks as SOPs.

For anything repetitive or complicated, I write it down as a step-by-step process. That way I never miss a detail, and the output stays consistent every time.

02

I take note of the details that matter.

Instructions, preferences, deadlines, edge cases — I log them as they come up instead of relying on memory, so nothing falls through the cracks.

03

I keep my workspace organized.

Files, assets, workflows, and deadlines all have a place. A clean virtual workspace means I'm never wasting time hunting for something I need.

04

I take the time to get it right.

I'd rather do a task carefully than rush it — that's how details get missed. Quality output comes first.

AI in My Workflow

I use tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini as part of my everyday workflow — for drafting, editing, research, organizing information, and quality-checking my own output. It helps me work more efficiently, catch human error before it reaches a client, and keep my output consistently high quality.
